The people behind the archive

This tribute brings together family recollections, historical photographs, local boxing history and previously assembled biographical research. Attribution matters: an image, a recollection, and a contemporary record are different kinds of evidence.

The Hanover family and other contributors preserve the memories that make this record possible. The photographs and documents in the public collection are presented as part of that shared remembrance. The life story provides the central narrative; the archive and photographs offer other ways to explore it.

Media and image attribution

The collection draws on the Eli Hanover Family Archives, family recollections, publicly accessible historical resources and independently hosted audiovisual works. Public online availability does not itself establish permission to reproduce an image or a recording.

  • Eli Hanover Family Archives: photographs, documents, family history and boxing memorabilia.
  • WMAR-TV / YouTube: references to Baltimore City Fair footage and regional television history.
  • East Baltimore historical materials: “Welcome Home,” a portrait of East Baltimore in 1975.
  • BoxRec: background reference for historical boxing records and athlete biographies.
  • Unsplash / Hugo Gogadjeto: Promoting image reference.
  • Unsplash / Jonathan Borba: Boxing gloves image reference.

Additional background imagery may have been sourced from Unsplash. The precise creator and asset should be confirmed before assigning individual attribution. Credits above refer to known source references and do not imply those works are all currently displayed.

Rights and corrections

Unless an individual item states otherwise, this site's original written content and arrangement are reserved. Third-party photographs, footage, music and artwork remain subject to their respective owners’ rights. Embedding a third-party video does not transfer ownership or reproduction rights.

If a photograph is misidentified, a credit is missing, or a rights holder requests a correction, please contact the website team. Corrections will be reviewed with reference to the historical record.
