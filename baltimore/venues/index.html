The venues behind fight night

Baltimore boxing drew its energy from local gyms, halls and neighborhood audiences.

The biographical account connects Eli Hanover with the Jewel Box, where boxing and entertainment crossed paths, and with events staged in local halls. Venue names and promotional dates require care: a surviving poster proves that an event was advertised, not necessarily every detail of its staging. The public boxing archive and promoter chapter provide the best starting points.
