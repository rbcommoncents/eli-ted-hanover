The Hanover family across generations

Family relationships provide the starting point for Eli’s story, from his parents’ immigration to the household he and Frances built in Baltimore.

Eli’s parents, Louis and Ella Hanover, settled in Baltimore after coming to the United States. Family history identifies Eli among seven children. Later, Eli and Frances raised their family in Baltimore. The photographs preserve many generations, but not every person and date has been identified; family connections should therefore be read alongside the more detailed Life Story.
