The life of Eli “Ted” Hanover

Five chapters following Eli from East Baltimore through boxing, the Merchant Marine, and family life.

Eli “Ted” Hanover’s life crossed the worlds of immigrant Baltimore, professional boxing, maritime work, and family. This chaptered account combines historical research with details passed down through the Hanover family.

The pages are working drafts. Where dates or recollections are not fully corroborated, the chapter notes make that clear.
