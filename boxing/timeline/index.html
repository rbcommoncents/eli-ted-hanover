A Baltimore boxing chronology

Eli Hanover’s boxing life belongs to a much longer history of the sport.

Family research places Eli’s early boxing activity in the late 1930s, followed by years in the Merchant Marine and later work around boxing promotion in Baltimore. The 1960s and 1970s brought new audiences and memorable fights. These broad periods form a useful chronology without assigning exact dates to undocumented individual events.
