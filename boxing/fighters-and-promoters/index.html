Fighters, promoters and the people around the ring

Baltimore boxing was built by athletes, trainers, matchmakers, promoters and the audiences who supported them.

Eli Hanover’s story intersects with a wider era of American boxing. The site’s 1960s–70s collection explores nationally known fighters alongside Baltimore’s local boxing scene; surviving posters and programs provide a closer view of advertised bouts and promotion work.
